2️⃣ Paragraph Chunking

এখানে separator="\n\n" ব্যবহার করা হয়েছে, তাই paragraph অনুযায়ী split হবে:

========== PARAGRAPH ==========

Chunk 1:
The detective entered the abandoned house just before midnight. The rooms were silent, and the windows were covered with dust. On the table, he found a bloodstained letter.
--------------------

Chunk 2:
The man who did the crime was John. Nobody in the village trusted him, but he had disappeared three days before the murder. The detective immediately realized that John was the main suspect.
--------------------

Chunk 3:
The next morning, the detective returned to the house. Behind a loose brick, he discovered a photograph of John standing beside the victim. This finally connected John to the crime.
--------------------

⚠️ এখানে paragraphs chunk_size=100 থেকে বড় হলেও CharacterTextSplitter সেগুলোকে automatically sentence-level-এ ভাঙে না।
